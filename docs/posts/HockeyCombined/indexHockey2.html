Unfortunately, despite all efforts to clean the data, some duplicate names remain. Notably, each of the duplicates that occurred come from the ‘Barrie’ portion of the dataframe, so we can manually add the results of his ballot to the others to get the final result.

In this case of the Hart Trophy, it does matter, since two 7 point ballots were not properly combined for Zach Werenski. The final score of 14 would be enough to take 5th place in this ballot over David Pastrnak. In addition, this provided an extra point for Nikita Kucherov, which places him in a tie for 3rd place with Shayne Gostisbehere. Nikita has one 1st and two 2nds among the voters, while Shayne has one 1st and only one 2nd, so Nikita will take third on a tiebreak.
